## Problem

The tutor had to stay on topic. Incorrect or off-topic answers were the gap I measured.

## Approach

Treat the off-topic rate as a number. Evaluation pipelines flagged unsafe or off-topic outputs, and 500+ test cases showed whether a change helped.

## Implementation

- Evaluation pipelines that catch unsafe or off-topic outputs.
- GPT models fine-tuned with guardrails.
- A RAG pipeline in front of the model. Documents are split with semantic chunking and stored in Milvus.
- An ingestion path that covered 10K+ documents.

## What I measured

- 80%+ fewer incorrect or off-topic answers across 500+ test cases
- 35%+ higher contextual accuracy after adding the RAG pipeline
- 40% lower retrieval latency after semantic chunking and Milvus ingestion

These figures come from internal evaluation during the internship in 2023. The test sets are not public.

## Current status

Shipped to 1,000+ beta users during the internship, which ended in August 2023.

## What I learned

A guardrail is only real if I measure it. The 500+ test cases were how I knew the off-topic rate had dropped.
