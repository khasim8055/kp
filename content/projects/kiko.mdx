## Idea

The goal is a small robot that feels like a companion and not like a gadget: it has a face, it recognises the person it belongs to, and it reacts to them.

## My part

The AI features, written in Python. Perception comes first, starting with recognising the owner from camera images.

## Current status

An early prototype on the bench. The face display, a camera and the compute board run together. The perception work is in progress, which is why the screen in the clip says the vision models are not installed.

There are no results to report yet, so this page has none.

## What I want to learn

Robotics is new for me. Kiko is how I am learning what changes when a model has to work on a real device, in a real room, in real time.
